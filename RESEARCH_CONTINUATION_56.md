# E56: Turku fragment C and a conflicting shelfmark

**E83 correction:** An attributed handlist names IV.156 as same book for the matching museum fragment C. The earlier IV.157 proposal is no longer preferred; the intended reference and physical join remain unresolved. [E83](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_83.md).

**E81 follow-up:** The Turku description's Henry-to-Purification contents are more readily compatible with the described IV.157 ff.2–3 interval than with the IV.156 ff.2–3 interval, subject to source, liturgical-order and bifolium assumptions. This remains a candidate interpretation, not a confirmed shelfmark correction or physical join. [E81](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_81.md).

31 October–1 November 2026. The preceding interrupted work made progress: it identified two current catalogue records and retained the scholarly TEI description. Both image downloads completed with certificate errors and no saved image bodies; they are not live processes.

The Turku City Museum's [TKM23245:3 record](https://museo.disec.fi/Yksa4/id/160988864803200/) closely matches [Codices Fennici fragment C](https://www.codicesfennici.fi/items/show/249): the descriptions share contents, dimensions and a credited Keskiaho description. This is a strong proposed crosswalk between records. The museum copies the scholarly description, so agreement is not independent corroboration.

The [older Helsinki catalogue](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1314) describes an uncatalogued museum Ms. c as a defective bifolium containing Henry and Purification material. Codices Fennici describes fragment C as two folios forming a damaged bifolium. The museum's count of one object can be consistent with two folios. The later indexed Helsinki description instead calls Ms. c one folio and names the provincial archive; those discrepancies remain unresolved.

Both the TEI and its museum copy literally give **F.m. II.157**, and attribute matching sewing holes and a possible placement between folios 2 and 3 to the catalogue. The indexed Helsinki IV.157 description places Henry material on folio 2 and Good Friday material on folio 3. IV.157 is a plausible intended reference, but the proposed correction has not been confirmed. No source photographs or holes were newly inspected. The placement and scribal attribution are published claims, not discoveries made here.
