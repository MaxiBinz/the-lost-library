## Efficiency and focus — 4 November 2026

The user requested a more focused, token-efficient strategy. The active discovery objective and its evidence standards remain in force; this is a prioritization of the existing investigation, not a claim that its scientific goal has been met or that all candidates are exhausted.

1. **First, test one accessible, decision-relevant question at a time.** The Turku *dedi[sti?]* / catalogue *redisti* observation (E85–E86) is the most concrete small correction lead. Check whether a genuinely new, authorized source or independent reading can resolve the initial letter and editorial policy; same-reader repetition is not independent confirmation. Even if confirmed, a small transcription correction must not be presented automatically as a comparably significant manuscript discovery.
2. **Second, pursue the strongest manuscript question only when a specific route offers decisive input.** For Fr 20106/8201, obtain a securely identified Fr 20106 record or image, or a source that resolves the precise textual/physical boundary; E105 identifies a historical online-access question. A missing or failed route is not negative historical evidence and must not be retried. Other existing leads remain available if they offer a specific feasible discriminator.
3. **Use a decision gate before each research pass.** Name the exact observation, source, possible outcomes and how each could change the conclusion. Avoid additional generic searches, repeat observations, model agreements, infrastructure or reports unless they could change that decision. Batch small source lookups, inspect excerpts rather than entire repetitive logs, and keep notes concise. Refresh larger packages when a substantive result needs review or at a checkpoint, rather than after every note.
4. **Aim for 15–30 minutes and roughly 10,000–20,000 tokens per focused pass; reassess after at most three passes.** These are soft planning targets, not an enforced budget or promise of a result. Report what changed, what remains inaccessible and whether another concrete test has sufficient value. Continue meaningful authorized work under the goal's existing rules; do not claim a fully exhausted negative result solely because the checkpoint elapsed.

No image upload, outside contact or publication is authorized by this efficiency adjustment. Independent review remains distinct from AI rechecking. The active goal should be continued, not replaced solely to implement this focus.
