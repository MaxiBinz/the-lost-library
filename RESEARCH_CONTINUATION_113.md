# E113 — different reuse descriptions for the apparent St James overlap

**4 November 2026.** This follows [E112](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_112.md). The comparison was selected after the passage and source assignments were known. It is not a new book attribution or an independently confirmed transcription.

The [Finnish National Library's indexed IV.156 catalogue](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1299?show=full) attributes to **f.8** an early modern heading naming Hindrich Jonszon and the Viborg garrison, with years **1603 and 1604**. The exact side marker and spelling come from OCR/search-indexed text and must be checked against the source; the Finnish leaf has not been examined. The retained BOMPAC workbook gives the exact f.8 row an account year of **1604**. This is a related catalogue-based data extraction, not an independent reading of the heading.

For **Fr 10406**, the retained original MPO catalogue says `Finska cameralia`, reference `71:2 1608`; the exact BOMPAC row gives account year **1608**. The [third retained photograph](data/images/mpo-10406-capture-3.jpg) has a visible date-like **1608** in its margin, read after a clockwise quarter-turn. Its author, date of inscription, function and relationship to the account are unverified. The [source-image selector](research/continuation_113/observations.json) records original pixels; no reconstructed date is written onto the image.

These descriptions make a simple assumption that the two records describe the same **single, unchanged account wrapper** less secure. Account years are not necessarily dates of binding: different accounts can be bundled, a parchment can be reused more than once or split, and either catalogue crosswalk or heading could be mistaken. The distinct record locations and reference strings do not prove distinct pieces of ink, exclude a shared medieval codex, or resolve whether the two *intercede* readings overlap. The evidence is a **reuse-context discriminator**, not a physical exclusion.

The exact included workbook rows, cell addresses, source checksums, Swedish CSV record and image region are in [results.json](research/continuation_113/results.json). The Finnish heading is a separate catalogue assertion; spelling and dates must not be silently promoted to a direct reading. Both records are already grouped as BOMPAC-5324, which is prior reconstruction metadata rather than evidence of their physical identity or of a new discovery.

Reproduce the local extraction and image provenance checks with `.venv/bin/python research/continuation_113/audit.py`. The script verifies the inputs, row identity, years and rectangle bounds, **not** the historical reading or which object carried the heading. The next decisive test remains source-identified Finnish f.8 images and the full account/context map. No source images were downloaded, no one was contacted, and no historical attribution or frozen decision changed.
