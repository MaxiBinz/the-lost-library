# E76 — a Lübeck control for the Fr 8073 chant sequence

2 November 2026. This is a reference-exposed comparison, not an independently reviewed transcription or a new same-book attribution.

## Why this control matters

The retained photographs of Fr 8073 provisionally show *Funde vinum desolatis* and its *Liberalis liberali* verse; a doxology and the hymn *Lucis huius festa*; and then the antiphon *Felix terra pariens*. The order across photographs is a working hypothesis, especially for capture 1 → 2; the 2 → 3 split-word reading is stronger, but folding and original sides remain unresolved. The [E75](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_75.md), [E74](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_74.md) and [E73](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_73.md) image regions preserve the separate readings.

Kerstin Schnabel's [catalogue description of Lübeck, Stadtbibliothek, Ms. theol. lat. 2° 48a](https://diglib.hab.de/?catalog=Schnabel&db=mss&id=hl-sb-theol-lat-2f-48a&list=ms) describes a separate, surviving collectar. In its St Anne section, ff. 69va–70va, it identifies the third responsory and verse of the second nocturn of *Analecta Hymnica* 5 no. 34, followed by a doxology. The [edited no. 34](https://de.scribd.com/document/66836506/Dreves-Analecta-Hymnica-Medii-Aevi-1886-Volume-V) identifies those two pieces as *Funde vinum* and *Liberalis liberali*. The catalogue then lists *Lucis huius festa* on f. 70va and *Felix terra* beginning there in the next section. This is a **catalogue-based parallel to the proposed arrangement**, involving a separate book, not a direct collation of its manuscript pages. The catalogue does not prove that every item is adjacent without intervening material, nor that the sung melodies match.

The description places the collectar's main writing space in two columns and gives its size as 33 × 24 cm; the MPO describes Fr 8073 as one column and approximately 43 × 32.5 cm, cut at top and bottom. These are catalogued descriptions, not a new physical measurement. The identified Lübeck book is a useful different-object control, not evidence for dividing the Swedish fragments into books or for their direct dependence on it.

The catalogue description was read through a full search-indexed result; a direct web-reader open failed. Its listed digital-facsimile resolver also returned a web-reader cache miss. Neither failed route was retried, and no Lübeck manuscript image was inspected or saved. Its detailed wording, stanza order, manuscript pitches and exact intervening material therefore remain unverified. The AH source and its modern extracts are related, not independent manuscript witnesses.

## Existing Ant 49 candidates

The retained MPO rows for all eight entries labelled Ant 49 were checked, including the previously missing-image entries Fr 20106 and Fr 20107. Of those rows, only Fr 8073 explicitly lists St Anne material; other rows describe dedication, common of virgins, Catherine/Clement/Barbara, or give no content. This is a **catalogue-description screen**, not a full transcription, and does not establish that St Anne material is absent from other fragments or that different feast texts imply different books. See the [reproducible row extraction](research/continuation_76/candidate_rows.json). No secure corresponding Anne passage on another existing candidate was identified in this screen.

## Implication

The three-work arrangement, even if the tentative Fr 8073 sequence is confirmed, cannot by itself identify one unique book: the published Lübeck description supplies a concrete alternative. This adds a stronger control than comparing any single common chant. It does not contradict the published Ant 49 associations or resolve original-side mapping. A useful further test must compare a distinctive, source-verified musical or textual detail, or a physical feature on **corresponding** material; it must include difficult different-book controls and should not assume identical chant order entails a shared codex. The prior *Celo ... sublata* reading and its variants remain unreviewed.

No new manuscript images were acquired, no historical lead decisions changed, and no original discovery or independent confirmation is claimed.
