## Research target: the IV.157-centred antiphonal family

The selected research question concerns the late-fifteenth-century Åbo/Nådendal antiphonal neighbourhood centred on **Helsinki, National Library of Finland, F.m.IV.157**, comparing **F.m.IV.156**, terminal leaf **F.m.IV.173**, and **Swedish Riksarkivet Codex1505: Fr8994,10370,10406,10407,10437**. Documented IV.157 associates **Fr1385 and CCM Ant99 (20274–20278)** supply comparison evidence. These identifiers were checked in the actual institutional export and Finnish catalogue; they are not invented model labels.

This is a **family/attribution audit, not one authenticated codex**. IV.157 lists14folios/28TIFF sides. Its catalogue dates the source c1460–1486, with an updated note c1460–1475, and proposes Åbo/Nådendal connections. Production origin is an attribution, not a settled fact. The surviving sources include music on red staves and an identifiable liturgical function, while qualified associations and shared-production explanations provide a concrete comparison question.

Verified small images exist for Fr1385,20274,8994 and the proposed Hy49/25592 comparator, plus a two-page PDF of IV.173. Further representative images are obtained by the bounded pipeline when accessible. Source-side dimensions, text/notation, capture geometry and catalogue uncertainty must be kept distinct.

**Novelty gate:** Keskiaho's *En grupp handskrifter från Nådendal: nya rön*, pp51–67, is identified (volume2018, author record2019) but unread. No current qualified reviewer response exists. Neither a known suggestion retrieved by the model nor a missing digital association establishes novelty. This limits an originality claim for the affected question; independent source tests and provisional arguments can continue.
