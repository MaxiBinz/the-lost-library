06 / SOURCES & LIMITS

Every fragment
has a provenance.

This atlas connects local analysis to the institutions and scholarship that make it possible. It does not replace their catalogues or specialist scrutiny.

The historical thread

During the Reformation, medieval parchment books were dismantled and reused as durable wrappers for administrative accounts. Fragment collections preserve both medieval music and traces of this later use.

Modern holding locations and account-book provenance do not automatically identify where a medieval manuscript was made. A reconstruction must follow the evidence across those different histories.

WHAT REMAINS UNCERTAIN

  • Catalogue associations require critical review.
  • Image similarity alone does not establish one manuscript.
  • A partial sequence does not supply missing pages or musical notes.
