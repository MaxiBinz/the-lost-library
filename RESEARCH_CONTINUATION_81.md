# E81: testing the Turku shelfmark against catalogue contents

**E83 correction:** An attributed handlist explicitly associates the corresponding museum fragment C with IV.156. The E81 folio-order comparison is conditional and no longer justifies preferring IV.157 as the intended reference. Both remain candidates; the handlist reproduction and underlying source need authentication. [E83](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_83.md).

**E82 follow-up:** Institutional series counts, a 2026 II.1–129 inventory and a bounded scan of the pinned BOMPAC workbook now strengthen the case that II.157 is a reference error in the ordinary current numbering system. The intended IV.157 emendation and the historical relationship remain unconfirmed. [E82](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_82.md).

2 November 2026. The 2017 [Codices Fennici description of Turku fragment C](research/continuation_56/TMMA_fragC.xml) twice prints **F.m. II.157**. It reports matching sewing holes and suggests a position between that book's folios 2 and 3; these are the cataloguer's claims, not new observations. E56 proposed **IV.157** as the intended shelfmark, without confirming the correction. This pass tests that proposal against another plausible reference, IV.156.

| Evidence from descriptions | IV.157 | IV.156 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finnish f.2 | Henry, ending at the fifth Lauds antiphon | Peter and Paul |
| Finnish f.3 | Good Friday | Visitation of Mary begins on ff.3–4 |
| Turku C | Henry ending on f.1r, followed in the description by Purification of Mary | Same description |

The [IV.157 catalogue](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1300) and [IV.156 catalogue](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1299?show=full) were available as **search-indexed text**, not fresh inspection of either Finnish leaf. The Turku description's surviving Henry ending is consistent with a second-Vespers antiphon in an [edition-derived Henry office](https://hlub.dyndns.org/projekten/webplek/CANTUS/cgi-bin/LMLO/LMLO.cgi?X=%5BHE61%5D), later in that edition's office than the Lauds antiphon described on IV.157 f.2. The Henry-to-Purification content is broadly compatible with the interval after IV.157 f.2 and before its f.3. The Peter-and-Paul-to-Visitation interval described for IV.156 is less straightforward under ordinary feast ordering.

This comparison sharpens **why IV.157 is a plausible intended referent**. It does not prove an exact continuation, that IV.156 is impossible, or that the original shelfmark is a typo. The actual manuscripts could differ from the edition, numbering or feast order could vary, and the two leaves of a bifolium do not automatically form one uninterrupted block. We did not inspect sewing holes or original images, and first notice of this comparison is unknown. An unsuccessful search and a blocked browse page do not prove that II.157 does not exist.

There is also an unresolved object crosswalk. The indexed Finnish entries describe an archive **Ms. c** as a single folio: IV.156 labels it from the same codex, whereas IV.157 presents it as suggested. Codices Fennici describes **museum fragment C** as a damaged bifolium. They may refer to the same object, but their holder and extent descriptions must be reconciled before the Finnish Ms. c statements can be transferred to museum C.

The IV.156 metadata also uses both a qualified Codex 1505 relation and a later equality-style IV.157 cross-reference. This wording was **already identified** in the project's [independent source-crosswalk audit](research/independent_pass/crosswalk/audit.md); it is not a new discovery, and it does not resolve the published BOMPAC split. E29's distinction between a suggestion and stronger labels remains useful but should be read with that cross-reference in view.

The next discriminating evidence is the original scholarly basis for the sewing-hole and placement statement, a reliable shelfmark inventory, and a documented object crosswalk; where lawfully accessible, compare the actual numbered leaves and their text. Record any alternative placement or source attribution before proposing a correction. No new attribution, exclusion, confirmed correction or publication results from this pass.
