# Research continuation E25: locating the official Taitto scan

**Date:** 24 October 2026. **Scope:** pursue a new, lawful route to the unread Taitto *Antiphonaria: text* volume (2001), including pages 161–168 cited by the Fr 8994 record.

## Result

The National Library of Finland's Open Data record identifies the text volume as an ebook and gives its digital-object URI, `URN:NBN:fi-fd2012-00000006`. The same record identifies the physical national-collection copy as `Ea 052952`; the text volume is catalogued as 253 pages plus five preliminary pages. An official Fragmenta record for F.m. IV.67 links to the National Library viewer with this digital-object identifier, demonstrating a catalogue-to-viewer route to the scan.

The exact viewer URL was requested for pages 161 and 168 through the available web reader, but both fetches returned `Cache miss`. No page image, OCR, or book text was retrieved. This records a limitation of this reader route only; it does not establish a rights restriction, universal unavailability, or that the viewer itself fails in a normal browser. The relevant passage remains unread, so this attempt changes no historical claim or novelty assessment.
