# E68 — locating Szilágyi's 1850 article

2 November 2026. Follow-up to the exploratory E67 crosswalk; the candidate was known before this search.

## Bibliographic finding

The Hungarian Academy's [repository record](https://real-j.mtak.hu/96/) lists István Szilágyi, “A mármaros-szigeti falfestésekről,” in *Magyar Academiai Értesítő* 10 (1850), pp. 234–258. Toldy's cited pp. 240 onward therefore fall within that catalogue range. The [Academy's later index](https://real-eod.mtak.hu/111/1/KIADV_075.pdf) also lists Szilágyi at volume 10 (1850), pp. 234–258, although it does not itself supply the title in the extract and its relationship to the catalogue data is unknown. The catalogue record and index favor **1850 as the volume date** over the 1861 almanac's 1851 reference; why the older almanac says 1851 is not established. Bibliographic agreement is not a direct reading of the article.

The repository offers a 32 MB scan, but one web-reader click on its download returned an error. The article itself was not read, no file was saved, and this route must not be repeated. The catalogue does not establish that its pages contain the quoted Catherine wording or the colophon.

## Context for the candidate

The [Névpont biographical entry](https://nevpont.hu/index.php/palyakep/szilagyi-istvan-3d129) describes Szilágyi as a teacher at the Máramarossziget Reformed gymnasium from 1845 to 1850 and its director thereafter, beginning in 1850. This is consistent with the author being connected to the school at the relevant period. Gábriel's 1942 description gives the historic depositor of no. 167 as the Máramarossziget Lyceum. The names and local institution suggest an institutional connection, but this biography does not identify either book, establish access to the breviary, prove that the named institutions are the same legal entity, or independently confirm the manuscript crosswalk. The sources may share historical dependencies.

## What remains to test

Seek a distinct legitimate copy of Szilágyi's pp. 240 onward and compare any colophon or ownership description against Gábriel's historical no. 167. An authoritative modern cross-reference or the original manuscript can also test the match. E67's candidate remains unresolved, as do modern custody, the medieval text, the Fr 20106 image, and the scientific novelty question. No new historical discovery, expert validation, source images, or downloads are claimed.
