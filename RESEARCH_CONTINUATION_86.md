- On the two bottom lines, the d in *odorem*, the d in the candidate *benedictus*, and the disputed initial have similar apparent tall oblique ascenders and bowls. The r forms in candidate *fructus* and *ventris* appear shorter and lack the same conspicuous ascender. The viewed rectangle was `xywh=839,2539,1901,1324`.

The r controls are medial, not word-initial: different position and ligatures may change their shapes. Their identification, the d controls and the target were all assessed by the same reader on the same image. No glyph statistics, original object examination or external correction is implied. The final part of the contested word is faded; the careful transcription remains **dedi[sti?]**. A familiar *dedisti* in another manuscript is context, not evidence for obscured strokes.

The initial-d case is stronger than in E85, but we retain **B / plausible** and keep the original catalogue form *redisti*, the proposed replacement, the uncertain ending and the question of first notice separate.
