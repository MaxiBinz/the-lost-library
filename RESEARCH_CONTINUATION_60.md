1 November 2026. E59 was progress: it added a relevant 2025 study and completed its verification. The exact Finnish IV.156 f.9 comparator remains uninspected. This turn tests a different, previously identified comparison within the same manuscript group: Fr 10370 versus IV.156 f.8.

## What can be compared

The uppermost preserved text on the [number-bearing Fr 10370 photograph](research/continuation_60/swedish-head.png) provisionally reads `[...]lene circa ministeria que sedens secus pedes domini audie[...]`. The following lines contain candidates for *Non est Martha* and *Et respondens*. The first passage fits *Satagebat igitur Martha*, Cantus 004816; a [search-indexed manuscript record](https://musmed.eu/chant/42833) and an [indexed 1537 source](https://cantusdatabase.org/chant/391041/) provide identification and sequence context. They are not transcriptions of Fr 10370. Our reading is unblinded, reference-assisted and incomplete; the opening starts within a word. The previous II/ij-like mark in that photograph and the k-like mark in another photo are **reused** observations, not new independent evidence. The catalogue treats the captures as one leaf; their complete original-side map remains unproved.

The [indexed IV.156 institutional description](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1299) describes f.8 with an opening-signature range approximately **[k] ijr – k [iij] verso** and says it begins at *ihesu* in the lauds antiphon *Haec corde compuncta*. Square brackets and OCR uncertainty matter: this is the catalogue's description, not a fresh reading of the Finnish page. The same record describes its numbering as an opening signature, with a letter on the verso and numeral on the facing recto. It does not make every apparent *k II* in a group an automatically unique leaf identifier.

## A testable conditional conflict

If the Swedish II-like mark and the indexed Finnish *k ij* identify the same unique original recto, both source beginnings are correctly and completely mapped, and the catalogue's boundary is accurate, the reported opening words differ. Under those combined premises the same-page interpretation requires revision. This sharpens a previously proposed comparison; it is **not a demonstrated historical exclusion**.

The alternatives include a mistaken or differently interpreted mark, an erroneous or OCR-damaged catalogue reading, a cropped or incorrectly mapped surface, reused or reset numbering, separate sections or a composite book. Both entries concern Mary Magdalene; different passages from that office on different leaves are normal. No Finnish manuscript image was examined and no original physical overlap was demonstrated. The [post-exposure protocol](research/continuation_60/protocol.json), [source regions](research/continuation_60/observations.json) and [structured result](research/continuation_60/results.json) preserve these conditions.
