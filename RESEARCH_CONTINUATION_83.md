# E83: an earlier attribution challenges the proposed shelfmark correction

**Bibliographic version clue, 4 November:** The references in the University of Turku publication [*Spreading the Written Word*](https://www.utupub.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/610e2dee-fa30-4a62-b6b9-8feb2a89e031/content) cite the same Keskiaho handlist title under **2013**, at the publisher's historical `http://www.finlit.fi/tutkimus/codices_handlist.pdf`. The previously identified 2015 thesis cites that title under **2014** at a different publisher path. These citations make multiple dated versions a possibility, but neither proves that the two citations refer to different content or identifies the date of either third-party reproduction. One ordinary web-reader request to the 2013 citation URL redirected to `https://www.finlit.fi/tutkimus/codices_handlist.pdf` and returned inaccessible; do not retry either exact route. We have not read the cited publisher file. The intended II.157 correction and competing IV.156/IV.157 readings remain unresolved.

**Source-access check:** The [publisher's EBSCO Historical Abstracts with Full Text coverage list](https://about.ebsco.com/m/ee/Marketing/titleLists/30h-coverage.htm) lists the journal's indexing from 1954 but full-text coverage only from October 2009. Keskiaho's relevant article is in **2008 volume 93, issue 3, pp. 318–350**; this specific advertised full-text route therefore does not cover its issue, even if an institution subscribes. EBSCO notes that coverage can change and other products or libraries may differ. This is not proof that the article is unavailable elsewhere or a reading of its contents. The [University of Helsinki catalogue](https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/fi/publications/kirjallinen-kulttuuri-keskiajan-suomessa/) and [Finnish library record](https://www.finna.fi/Record/kyyti.515646) identify the 2010 *Kirjallinen kulttuuri keskiajan Suomessa* as a 480-page book; they do not expose its relevant pages 418–419. One web-reader request to the Doria BOMPAC collection URL `https://doria.fi/handle/10024/184215` returned a cache miss; it must not be retried. No manuscript claim or novelty decision changed.

**4 November follow-up:** A [second third-party indexed handlist copy](https://abcdocz.com/doc/3468619/jesse-keskiaho-codices-fennici.-kartoitus-suomessa-ennen-...) also describes fragment C as from IV.156. Its entry ends with note number 83, rather than 87 in the previously read copy. This shows a textual or display difference between the reproductions; their dates, editorial relationships and fidelity to an authenticated publisher copy remain unknown. They are attributed to the same author on the same third-party host and are **not independent scholarly evidence** or proof of the physical relationship. The second copy's item was seen in the search index, not a fully opened page. Four parallel `find` requests inadvertently fetched its identical URL after the first inaccessible outcome; all returned cache misses. That repeated access violated the project's no-retry rule. Stop using that URL. The 2008 article and publisher handlist remain unread, and neither the intended TEI emendation nor a book attribution is established.

2 November 2026. **We withdraw the preference for IV.157 as the intended correction.** A handlist attributed to Jesse Keskiaho instead explicitly associates the same described Turku museum fragment C with **IV.156**. This is meaningful contrary evidence; neither IV.156 nor IV.157 has been established as the intended reference in the later description.

In a [third-party HTML reproduction of the Codices Fennici handlist](https://abcdocz.com/doc/3451205/jesse-keskiaho-codices-fennici.-kartoitus-suomessa-ennen-...), item 19 describes a two-folio antiphonary fragment C in the Turku provincial museum archive, with Henry and Purification material. Its Finnish wording says the fragment is from the same book as Helsinki F.m. IV.156. Note 87 cites Keskiaho 2008b, p. 326. The shared museum, identifier, extent and contents make this a strong **descriptive** crosswalk to the fragment C in the [2017 TEI description](research/continuation_56/TMMA_fragC.xml); no object was newly examined. The Finnish wording was interpreted with AI assistance and has not been independently translated.

The handlist is attributed to the cataloguer, but the exact date and fidelity of this third-party reproduction remain unverified. A [University of Turku thesis](https://www.utupub.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/d93fc5c8-bf5e-4670-9fef-c8c4eb414d14/content) cites a 2014 handlist under the same title and a historical Finnish Literature Society URL. One web-reader attempt to open that publisher URL reported it inaccessible and was stopped. We have not authenticated that the reproduced text is the exact 2014 version, nor read the cited 2008 article page. These copies and the later TEI may rely on common work and are not independent physical observations.

E81's relative folio-content comparison gave IV.157 a more natural fit **if** the TEI's proposed folios-2/3 position and ordinary ordering are both correct. The handlist gives IV.156 a specific, directly attributed prior relationship to the matching museum description. We cannot assume that the handlist's book and the later TEI's intended reference are identical: a changed attribution, transcription error, different numbering or an error in the folio placement remain possibilities. Therefore **neither candidate should be ranked as the intended correction on current evidence**. The [E82 inventory](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_82.md) still makes the literal II.157 difficult to reconcile with normal current numbering, but does not pick its replacement.
