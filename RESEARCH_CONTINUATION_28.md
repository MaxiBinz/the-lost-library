# Research continuation E28: the broader Naantali fragment group in prior scholarship

**Date:** 24 October 2026. **Question:** does the E27 catalogue/workbook crosswalk reveal a real scholarly disagreement, or do its sources describe relationships at different levels?

## Finding

An institutional research-portal record identifies Ville Walta's *Birgittine Fragments in Finland: Reconstructing Naantali Abbey's Library* as a peer-reviewed 2013 chapter (pp. 11–29). The indexed full-text excerpt for the chapter's discussion of the broader group (printed p. 20, footnote 35) says that a larger group of up to 19 manuscripts shares striking layout, script or decoration traits. Its cited members include F.m.IV.156, IV.157 and IV.173, alongside other Finnish and Swedish fragments. The excerpt describes several different scribal hands and says only one or perhaps two items can be directly connected to Naantali; production at the abbey therefore remains uncertain and could have occurred elsewhere. The chapter also says that Keskiaho added fragments to the group in 2010, pp. 418–419. See the [institutional publication record](https://researchportal.helsinki.fi/en/publications/birgittine-fragments-in-finland-reconstructing-naantali-abbeys-li/) and [chapter PDF](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2%3A668618/FULLTEXT01.pdf).

This makes the E27 result clearer. The official Fragmenta record distinguishes Ant 242/Fr 29666 as “from same codex” from the suggested Hy 49/Fr 25592 and Ant 99 relations, while discussing a much wider stylistic/liturgical group. BOMPAC assigns a smaller three-row reconstruction unit to IV.173, Fr 29666 and Fr 25592, and keeps Ant 99/IV.157 in another unit. Walta's broader group contains both IV.173 and IV.157, but does not identify every member as one codex. The apparent difference is therefore not evidence that the publications contradict each other: they may be describing a same-codex reconstruction, suggestions, and a broad production/style group at different levels. The exact relation still needs source-by-source comparison.
