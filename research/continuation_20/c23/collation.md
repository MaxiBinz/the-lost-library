# Fr 20274 compared with actual C23 source pages

23 October 2026. Two actual source images from Uppsala University Library's [C23 record in Alvin](https://www.alvin-portal.org/alvin/view.jsf?pid=alvin-record:160517) now permit a comparison beyond the edition. **The retained sites support agreement with current corrected/common wording; they establish no distinctive shared uncorrected C23 error.** They do not establish a copying relationship, direction of transmission, common exemplar or physical manuscript association.

## Sources and selection

The exact institutional TIFF attachments 0144 and 0145 and their recorded 2200-pixel derivatives were inspected. Derivative hashes and paths are in [collation.json](collation.json); acquisition/rights receipts are separate. Attachment 0144's text corresponds to the office opening Hallqvist identifies as C23 f.71v. No original folio numeral was independently read on that image. Attachment 0145 has a visible pencilled 72, continues the text and agrees with the edition's 72r transition. Image ordinal alone does not establish medieval sides.

The complete relevant Hallqvist page, printed p.20/PDF p.67, was visually inspected, including its critical apparatus. Ten sites were retained from that page, including two whose target text is unavailable. This list was set before inspection of attachment 0145, after earlier inspection of 0144 and the target. The comparison is post-review and descriptive, without a blind or preregistered claim. No disagreeing or unavailable site is removed from the report.

The seven current word/cue agreements, one difference of written scope and two missing target sites are a descriptive inventory. They are not independent evidence channels or an accuracy estimate. In particular, an inherited original error could be informative, but convergence on standard wording after correction is not by itself a transmission fingerprint.

## Observation and editorial report remain distinct

Current C23 corrections are directly visible. Complete cancelled wording is often less legible; the apparatus supplies some original-state readings. Those editorial reports must remain labelled as reports, including the attribution of another correcting hand. No C435 original image was inspected here. The edition's corrected text, the source's present state and reconstructed prior states are separate evidence layers.

The 12 source-region observations comprise nine C23 regions and three additional Fr 20274 regions, with exact pixels, source hashes, literal candidates and limitations in [observations.json](observations.json). Earlier target observations are referenced, not overwritten. C23 pages are external comparison sources, not new attribution candidates or additions to the ten-pair frozen queue.

The narrow hypothesis of a distinctive uncorrected C23 fingerprint is **not supported by these retained visible sites**. This does not exclude a wider relationship or supply a stemma. No melody, rare-variant frequency, performance practice, authorial priority or new physical attribution was established. All source readings remain provisional AI readings without independent expert adjudication.
