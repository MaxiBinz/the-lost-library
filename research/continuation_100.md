# E100 — checking the Sziget breviary reference

3 November 2026. Follow-up to E67–E68, selected after the candidate and bibliography were already known. This check did not establish a new manuscript relationship.

The search-indexed text of [Toldy's 1855 edition](https://mek.oszk.hu/17000/17077/17077.pdf), introduction p. IX, explicitly cites István Szilágyi's article on Máramarossziget wall paintings, in the Academy journal for 1850, pp. 240 onward, for the 1399 Sziget breviary. The subject of the article's title is therefore not evidence that E68 attached the wrong citation. This reconfirms the attribution already recorded in E68; it is not independent corroboration of Szilágyi's claims about the book. The [Academy catalogue](https://real-j.mtak.hu/96/) places the article at pp. 234–258. Szilágyi's article itself remains unread, and no direct historical link between Toldy's book and Gábriel's historical no. 167 has been established.

A search for modern descriptions also returned material about a Dominican convent on a different Sziget (island), including a [2022 study](https://real-j.mtak.hu/22358/32/MNy_2022-3_szines.pdf) discussing a 1494 printed Venetian breviary. Its search extract does not identify the Máramarossziget 1399 manuscript and must not be merged with it on the word “Sziget” alone. The search did not find an authoritative cross-reference or current shelfmark; this is not proof that none exists.

**Access log and correction to procedure.** The direct URL for Toldy's PDF was inadvertently opened again despite E67 recording its prior direct-open failure. It again returned an error; stop and do not retry this route. Search-indexed text was separately available and is the basis of the citation check above. A web-reader find on a separate, large Academy-hosted study of old wall paintings returned a content-too-large error; no text was obtained from that request and that failed route should not be repeated. No downloaded source files or manuscript images were retained; web traffic is unmeasured within the existing reserve.

The next discriminating evidence remains a legitimately accessible, distinct copy of Szilágyi's cited pages, an authoritative cross-reference connecting them to no. 167, or the relevant manuscript text and current catalogue identification. A search result about another Sziget or an agreement in date alone is insufficient. The original Fr 20106 remains uninspected. No originality, physical identity, new historical discovery, expert validation, external contact, or publication is claimed.
