# E104 — the liturgical structure behind the E103 contrast

**3 November 2026.** Prior-art and alternative-explanation check on the existing Fr 8201 comparison; exploratory and reference-exposed.

## What the sources show

On Fr 8201, the locally observed *Specie corporis* ending is followed by *Traditur ergo* and then a *Nobilis* responsory, with short cues between them. The fragment does not show the beginning or the full number of antiphons in this nocturn. The chant identifications and literal readings remain provisional, and the Fr 20106 side is uninspected. See [E65](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_65.md), [E66](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_66.md) and [E103](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_103.md).

In a published comparison of Catherine offices, Czesław Grajewski, [“Gratulemur in honore” (2020), pp. 111–112](https://doi.org/10.21852/sem.2020.1.08), places *Specie*, *Traditur* and *Nobilis* in that order under the first nocturn in each of three comparison columns. The columns group descriptions of different Płock-associated and comparative sources; the table does not establish textual or documentary independence among them. Read via [the journal text](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/496828.pdf), not the original medieval sources. Grajewski is also the author of the previously discussed 2022 Fr 20106/8201 proposal; this is not an independent expert review of our reading.

E103's [Cantus-indexed Einsiedeln 611](https://cantusdatabase.org/chant/232925/) instead puts the same pair in positions A1.2 and A1.3, then three further antiphons in A1.4–A1.6, before R1.1 *Nobilis*. Cantus describes that witness as monastic. Michelle Urberg's [2016 dissertation](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/ejwkr-nxd77), printed p. 29, table 1.2, compares a six-antiphon monastic first nocturn with other shorter arrangements in **ferial** examples. Its general scheme provides context, not a direct transcription of the Catherine feast or a classification of Fr 8201.

## Interpretation and limits

The contrasting position of *Nobilis* is consistent with the number of antiphons used before the first responsory in the respective liturgical arrangements. This is a plausible structural explanation of E103's contrast, not a proof that this distinction alone caused every difference. The published shorter-form comparisons also show that Fr 8201's short observed order is already documented in other sources; the three-chant order is not evidence for one unique parent manuscript, one place or one institutional setting. Different offices and transmitted versions can share components.

Neither the complete first nocturn of Fr 8201 nor the original Einsiedeln pages were inspected here. Do not assign a firm cursus, geographic origin, numerical prevalence or independent manuscript relationship based on these short passages. This does not reject or establish the published Fr 20106/8201 relationship, and it is not a new historical discovery. The practical next discriminator remains the original Fr 20106 and its physical extent, side and exact continuation, combined with independent codicological evidence and prior-art checks.

The journal article was consulted as extracted text; no article PDF or image was retained. The Urberg table was read from the existing private research text, not redistributed. Search traffic is unmeasured within the existing reserve. No source images were acquired, no one was contacted, and no formal experiment or frozen candidate was changed.
