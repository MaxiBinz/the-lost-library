# E73 — identifying the St Anne material on Fr 8073

2 November 2026. Reference-assisted, exploratory reading of existing photographs, not an independent transcription. This addresses the work identification left open in [E52](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_52.md).

## A Magnificat antiphon

The retained [Fr 8073, capture 3](research/continuation_52/images/Fr8073-capture-3-native.jpg) contains the red rubric *Super magnificat antiphona*. Beneath it, the text provisionally reads *Felix terra pariens ... in quo corporaliter fons divinitatis ... reos a peccatis solvens duc ad patriam summae claritatis*. The candidate full text and exact image bounds are recorded in the [annotation](research/continuation_73/observations.json); letter forms, abbreviations, and red additions need independent checking.

The [Cantus Index entry 201813](https://cantusindex.uwaterloo.ca/id/201813) identifies the corresponding text as an antiphon for St Anne. Its search-indexed text was available; direct access returned a cache miss. An OCR extraction of [Analecta Hymnica 5, no. 34, p. 107](https://de.scribd.com/document/66836506/Dreves-Analecta-Hymnica-Medii-Aevi-1886-Volume-V) also prints it at the Magnificat within an Anne office. OCR and a reference text are not the Swedish original, and no melody has been transcribed. This establishes a qualified, locally image-supported work identification, not a new composition or codex attribution.

## A hymn and a documented textual alternative

The [same catalogued fragment, capture 4](research/continuation_52/images/Fr8073-capture-4-native.jpg), contains a verse provisionally read *Annam filiam Abrahe que fulsit ex Aaron stirpe*. This fits the second stanza of *De stella sol oriturus*, **Analecta Hymnica 52, no. 104**, already listed in the existing MPO catalogue description for Fr 8073. The image reading does not assume a recto/verso assignment or physical order between captures.

An [OCR extract of AH 52, no. 104](https://de.scribd.com/doc/66837361/Dreves-Analecta-Hymnica-Medii-Aevi-1886-Volume-LII) gives the corresponding words in a different order, *quae ex Aaron stirpe fulsit*. [Henryk Piwoński's 1985 edition, p. 305](https://bibliotekanauki.pl/articles/1044076.pdf), prints *quae fulsit ex Aaron stirpe*, agreeing with our provisional reading. This wording is therefore **already published**. Piwoński lists several sources for the hymn; we did not inspect their manuscripts or establish which particular witness underlies this line of his edition. The local reading and the two edited texts make a traceable comparison, not a claim that this word order is unique to the Swedish fragment or evidence of direct copying.

## What the comparison can answer

The previously tentative *Felix terra* candidate can now be compared with other actual witnesses at the same liturgical position. A candidate same-book relation would require a corresponding passage, diagnostic physical or musical agreement, and controls. An unrelated feast or different text elsewhere on a fragment is not by itself a contradiction. We have not established a same-book or different-book decision for Fr 8073 and any Ant 49 member, nor a geographic origin, independent confirmation, or a scientifically novel finding.

The source is a historical wrapper reused for a Reval account; that reuse location is not automatically the book's place of production. The current comparison is unblinded and no additional manuscript image was acquired. The official source photograph's native request for capture 1 previously failed and was not retried. Several new reference opens failed; [access notes](research/continuation_73/sources.json) record them, including three simultaneous accidental repeats of one unsuccessful page request. The goal and all existing historical decisions remain unchanged.
