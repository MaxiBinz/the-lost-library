# E63: a controlled D-initial comparison for IV.173 and Fr 1385

**Result:** the selected initials share a broad decorative vocabulary, but that vocabulary is also visible in a published different-book control. It cannot establish that IV.173 and Fr 1385 came from one book. The target image is too small to assess finer detail reliably. The case remains unresolved; no new attribution, exclusion or discovery is claimed.

## Source test

We compared the D starting *Dignare* on the retained second photograph of Helsinki F.m.IV.173 with the candidate D starting *Dabit* in Fr 1385's first retained photograph. Both have a red upright and pale right field; IV.173's D has two apparent red diamond-like interior marks. Fr 1385's original local thumbnail is only 600 pixels wide: a colored mark is visible, but exact count, color and fine outline cannot be judged reliably. The IV.173 letter marks a verse within the *Te Deum*, while equivalence of decorative function with Fr 1385 has not been established.

For a same-letter counterexample we reused the documented E20 D regions of Fr 8201 and Fr 8304. The published assessment associates these with the same hand but different books; their selected initials repeat red, pale and decorative-mark features. This is a useful difficult negative for the general vocabulary, not a matched estimate of a feature's prevalence. It does not show that IV.173 and Fr 1385 are different books.

A source-documented same-book positive for Fr 1385 would be Fr 20274 / Ant 99, but its two retained overlapping views do not present a comparably decorated D. This is an image-coverage limitation, not a statement that the letter is absent from the manuscript. The IV.173 same-codex comparator Fr 29666 is also unavailable as a local image. Different roles, decorators, sections, composition, image quality and possible errors remain alternatives. The selection follows earlier exposure and is exploratory; no classifier, accuracy estimate or independent reading is implied.

## Access, traceability and next step

We made one bounded request for the native Fr 1385 photograph using the exact painting URL in its previously saved public-domain-marked IIIF manifest. Local DNS resolution failed; no bytes were received, and the route was not retried. This is an environment-specific access outcome, not evidence that the source does not exist. The two exact source regions, hashes, roles and limits are in [observations.json](research/continuation_63/observations.json), with the [protocol](research/continuation_63/protocol.json) and [receipt](research/continuation_63/download_receipt.json). The control regions remain in [E20 observations](research/continuation_20/ornament/observations.json). No new image or external communication occurred.

A stronger test would combine an ordinarily accessible higher-resolution target, role-matched examples from a documented same-book comparator, and independent page-order, signature, textual or physical evidence. Prior scholarship remains incompletely read, so novelty is not cleared. The goal remains active.
