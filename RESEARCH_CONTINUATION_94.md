# E94: A St Olaf comparison that limits the matching claim

**3 November 2026.** The existing Swedish Codex 1505 grouping is published prior work. This unblinded, reference-assisted comparison asks whether the St Olaf text on Fr 10406 supplies a more specific physical match to Fr 8994 or to a Finnish fragment. It does not.

## What the sources say

On [Fr 10406 photograph 3](data/images/mpo-10406-capture-3.jpg), the provisional reading of the decorated passage fits *Adest dies leticie*, the Magnificat antiphon of St Olaf's first vespers. On [photograph 4](data/images/mpo-10406-capture-4.jpg), *Magnus dominus et laudabilis valde ... qui regis Olaui industria* fits the invitatory for matins. The [published St Olaf edition](https://consortiumvocale.no/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/LWC1106_Historia_Sancti_Olavi_ebooklet_lowres.pdf) supplies these identifications and their office positions; it is not an independent transcription of this Swedish leaf. The readings are preliminary AI observations and require a competent independent reader. Fr 8994's previously recorded *Nec propria salute contentus* and *Egregius martyr Olavus* belong to the later third nocturn in the same reference. See [E33](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_33.md) and [E40](RESEARCH_CONTINUATION_40.md) for the original Fr 8994 regions and their limitations.

The [Finnish National Library catalogue for F.m. IV.33](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/221?show=full), read through search-indexed text, describes folios 1–8 as including Mary Magdalene, James, Anne and Olaf, ending in the *Magnus dominus* invitatory. This is a useful contextual comparator for the Magdalene/James/Olaf sequence in the [Swedish catalogue's Fr 10406 record](data/fragments.json), but the catalogued Anne office has not been established on Fr 10406. We did not inspect the Finnish source images. The Finnish description does **not** say which word the surviving invitatory ends on; photograph 4 of Fr 10406 ends within the song, which also does **not** establish the physical end of that leaf. An exact common endpoint is therefore **not established**.

The Finnish catalogue describes IV.33 as a 14th-century, Nordic Dominican antiphoner with reported dimensions 16–16.5 × 21.5–22 cm and a letter-and-Arabic-numeral foliation in text ink. Fr 10406 is catalogued as 15th-century, with reported leaf dimensions 43 × 34 cm and written space 35.5 × 23 cm; Fr 8994's directly inspected numeral and letter are red, although their full opening and Finnish side assignments remain unsettled. These descriptions are not physical measurements by this project, and the precise comparison of the Finnish dimensions needs verification against its images and cataloguing convention. They give substantive reasons to question a simple identification, **not** an independently confirmed exclusion. Similar texts in apparently different sources cannot be treated as proof of one book.

The [IV.156 catalogue](https://fragmenta.kansalliskirjasto.fi/handle/10024/1299?show=full) explicitly cites Codex 1505, including Fr 10406 and Fr 8994, as a suggested association. BOMPAC has already grouped them with IV.156. Neither the shared office nor their published association is a new discovery. The same catalogue describes IV.156's St Anne observance as occurring later in its calendar than Olaf; the presence, absence, or position of an Anne office in the Swedish book remains unverified. The IV.33 description is a comparison, not an expansion of the frozen candidate queue or a newly established attribution.

## Result and next discriminating evidence

This pass adds identifiable St Olaf passages to the source-linked dossier, but there is no demonstrated physical join, new book relationship, or claim of originality. [Structured observations](research/continuation_94/comparison.json) preserve the image URLs, hashes, regions and limits. The source images and the Finnish catalogue description are distinct forms of evidence; the common chant and office sequence are related, not independent votes.

A stronger test remains the exact passage and opening-signature comparison with IV.156 or IV.157 source images, coupled with capture-side mapping and a controlled check against genuinely separate books. Those Finnish originals have not been read here; earlier access failures must not be retried or bypassed. The already identified independent-project limitations and unread scholarship remain. No external contact, upload, download or publication occurred in this pass.
